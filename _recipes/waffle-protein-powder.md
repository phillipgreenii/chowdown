ingredients:
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1 large whole egg  
- 1 large egg white
- 1/2 cup greek yogurt
- 2 scoop protein powder (vanilla)
- 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 pinch kosher salt 
- 1 teaspoon honey
- 1/4 cup crushed nuts or semi-sweet and/or dark chocolate chips
- butter and syrup for topping

directions:
- Spray stuffed waffle maker with non-stick spray
- Turn on stuffed waffle maker
- Blend all of your ingredients together until smooth
- Pour batter into stuffed waffle maker until just touch square peaks
- Add stuffing (nuts and/or chocolate chips) into center of stuffed waffle maker
- Pour battrer into stuffed waffle maker until top of rings
- Cook until done (green light will turn on)
- Carefully remove from stuffed waffle maker and top with syrup and butter as desired

notes:
- pay attention to stuffed waffle maker, it may start to pop up from the expansion of the batter.  If so, you may need to hold lid closed to ensure proper cooking.
